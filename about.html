The Founder

Petr Pycha

MUSA Ltd. was established by Petr Pycha with a clear focus: to deliver orchestral recordings that meet the standards of the world's most demanding composers and productions. Two decades on, the work covers considerable ground — films presented at major international festivals, video game soundtracks reaching tens of millions of players worldwide, and studio albums recorded with artists including Celine Dion, Sarah Brightman, Helloween, and Joss Stone.

The Approach

Professional Execution,
Direct Communication.

Today, MUSA operates from Prague with a selected team of conductors, engineers, and orchestrators, working alongside the FILMharmonic Orchestra — a contracted ensemble of the city's most experienced recording musicians. Every project is handled with the same discipline: professional execution, direct communication, and a finished result the composer and production team can stand behind.
